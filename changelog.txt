## hibiki 3.0.0

**One app on phone and computer**
- hibiki is now built as one app for Android and Windows, with the same sources, the same library and the same player. The Android app is rebuilt on it: it updates the old one in place and carries over its library and watch progress on first start.
- Your devices keep each other up to date over the local network: library, progress and ratings follow you from the computer to the phone and back, with the titles' names and posters. If the other device lacks a source the data needs, hibiki offers to install it. Removing a device ends the pairing on both sides.

**Sources**
- Sources now come from repositories instead of being built in. hibiki-sources and the Aniyomi repository are there from the start, more can be added by a link to the repository on GitHub, and adding one asks you to confirm that you trust it.
- A source that stops at a Cloudflare check no longer just fails: the error offers to pass the check in a separate window and tries again.

**Player**
- When an episode comes with several audio tracks (Alloha), you can choose one in the player settings.
- A stream that does not start or breaks mid-way hands over to the next link by itself.
- Subtitles can be moved anywhere on the screen and keep clear of the controls, and the screen orientation is chosen in the player's own menu.

**Interface**
- The phone layout is redone: home with a swipeable hero and lists made for a thumb, a catalog in a grid, a compact title page and bottom sheets for filters and choices.
