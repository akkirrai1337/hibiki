## hibiki 3.2.0

**Player**
- Sleep timer in the player's menu: 15, 30, 45 minutes, an hour, or the end of the episode. When it runs out playback pauses; "end of episode" lets the episode finish without starting the next one.
- Stable volume (same menu): loud scenes and quiet dialogue are evened out, so there is less reaching for the volume at night.
- Picture enhancement (Anime4K) no longer freezes the player when it is turned on: it runs off the main thread, a badge says it is starting, and a device that cannot keep up steps down from strong to light to off by itself.
- Optional Anime4K upscaling for pictures under 1080p, and a hint about RTX Video Super Resolution on NVIDIA RTX cards.
- With auto-skip on, loading stops a few seconds into an opening or ending and resumes after the skip, so the part you skip is not downloaded.

**Less traffic**
- New Settings > Network: data saver, home refresh, player buffer, Wi-Fi-only downloads and autoplay (phone), parallel downloads, source request timeout, DNS-over-HTTPS and proxy (desktop), Cloudflare reset, cache size, and a per-day traffic chart.
- Data saver "auto" picks the starting quality from the measured speed of the connection (1080p, 720p, 480p or 360p).
- Video you have already watched is kept on the device for a day (up to 200 MB): rewinding far back, reopening an episode or going back to a quality you used before no longer downloads it again. It is counted and cleared with the rest of the cache.
- On mobile data or with data saver on, the player reads less ahead (10 or 15 seconds instead of 20 or 40) and follows a change of network in the middle of an episode.
- The player never downloads a quality taller than the picture it shows, and while the picture is not seen (audio only on the phone, a hidden window on desktop) it drops to the lightest one.
- HEVC and AV1 versions are preferred over H.264 of the same height when a source offers them.
- Home rows and filter lists are saved on the device and reused instead of downloaded again; how often they refresh is up to you (every launch, daily, every 3 days, manually), and longer on mobile data. Search and catalog pages are not downloaded again when you come back from a title.
- Source pages are asked about instead of downloaded again: a page that has not changed costs a few bytes. A title page reuses its details for 6 hours and its episode list for 10 minutes.
- Android sources accept Brotli compression, as the Aniyomi ones already did.

**Android**
- The app opens on a dark screen with the same pulsing pink dot as on the computer, instead of a grey one, and the dot stays until the first screen is ready.
- Wi-Fi-only downloads, Wi-Fi-only autoplay and the longer storage of home on mobile data now actually work: the app could not tell mobile data from Wi-Fi before.

**Profile**
- A picked profile picture opens a full-screen editor first: move it, zoom with a pinch, the mouse wheel or the slider, turn it by a quarter, and pick the part inside the square frame. Animated GIFs skip it and keep moving.

**Aniyomi sources (Android)**
- Extensions built for the newest Aniyomi extensions library (versions 14 to 17) install and run, including those that keep extra data on their videos and hosters.
