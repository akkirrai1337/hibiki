## hibiki 3.3.0

**Profile**
- Watch statistics: a radar of how you watch - binging, consistency, finishing what you start, variety, night watching, ongoing titles - with a switch to your six most watched genres. Hover or tap a corner to see what it was measured from.
- Beside it, the plain numbers: episodes and minutes on a day you watch, days with watching, the most episodes in a day, the longest streak, titles finished and dropped, your average rating, the hour you watch most and your usual dub. On the phone they sit under the radar as tiles.
- Your avatar is the profile entry in the tab bar and the sidebar.

**Player**
- Subtitle style: size (75-200%), font, colour and what sits behind the letters - the dark plate, a lighter one, an outline or a shadow. Shown live on the video and in a sample.
- Subtitle timing: when the lines run ahead of or behind the speech, move them by 0.1 or 0.5 s in Subtitles > Timing offset, or with Z and X on the desktop.
- Skip buttons for openings and endings on many more episodes: when the source marks none, the times come from AniSkip.
- The settings menu works while the player is still loading, and the list of players shows at once instead of waiting for a stream - a slow or stuck player can be switched away from straight away. A player you pick stays picked.
- A player whose site is down says so and offers Retry, instead of "This player isn't supported".
- A stream that expires is renewed from its own player, without reloading the episode's whole list; switching players back and forth plays the same stream again.
- A paused video shows the right subtitle line after a seek.
- "Auto" quality stops at 720p on mobile data.

**Downloads**
- Download a whole title (season) at once: dub, quality and player picked once, all or only unwatched episodes, already downloaded ones left out.
- A downloads indicator in the title bar (and next to search on the phone) shows what is downloading from anywhere, with a panel to pause, resume, cancel or retry.
- The downloaded episodes screen is redesigned: a card per title, full-width episode rows with progress and a delete button that also works on the phone. How much they take is in Settings > Network.
- Two titles whose episodes share a number no longer get in each other's way in the queue.

**Home and title pages**
- "Because you watched X" rows on the home screen, from the site's own "similar" lists.
- The title page shows the title and its episodes first and the recommendations after, instead of waiting for the slowest request.
- Episode lists are kept per title until the details say something changed, so opening a title and starting episodes downloads much less.
- Posters are fetched at the size the card shows (a catalog scroll on the phone uses about a third of the traffic).
- A title keeps the cover its card had when the details come without one.

**New-episode notifications (Android)**
- Ongoing titles in "Watching" (or any title with the bell on) are checked for new episodes in the dub you watch, and a notification opens the episode. The bell is on the title page and in Settings > General. Checks run while the app is open.

**Aniyomi sources on the desktop**
- Aniyomi APK sources now run on Windows, macOS and Linux too, bundled with the app (no Java to install): install them from repositories like on the phone, with Cloudflare checks passed in the app's window.

**Device sync**
- Changes are no longer lost when they land mid-sync or after restoring a backup, and received rows are not sent straight back.
- Pairing works only while the pairing window is open; the code changes every 2 minutes and five wrong tries close the window. "Forget this device" reaches the other device at once.

**macOS and Linux**
- Builds for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Linux (AppImage), with a one-command install script, and updates that replace the app in place.
- On macOS the window has no system title bar; the traffic lights sit over the app's own.

**Updates**
- Updates are picked from a manifest in each release and verified by checksum on the desktop.
