## hibiki 2.8.0

**Sources**
- Aniyomi APK extensions are now the only kind of source: they can be installed, updated and configured in the app, and their filters, sorts, hosters, DASH streams and skip timestamps are supported.
- Extensions installed outside Hibiki are detected and trusted by the repository they come from.
- Added an offer to move a library from the retired JS sources to their extension counterparts, also reachable from Settings.
- Removed the external metadata aggregators.

**AniList**
- Added AniList library sync: sign-in with OAuth or by username, import into Hibiki and sending the library back with a confirmed preview.
- Added episode progress, a skip-18+ setting, sync on launch (at most every 12 hours) and a Home sync button with a pending-changes banner.
- Removing a synced title asks whether to delete it only in Hibiki or on AniList too.
- Added an AniList step to the first-launch onboarding.

**Home and details**
- Merged continue watching and recently watched into one row of last-frame cards with a shared transition into the title page.
- Added the source's own filters to Home and search, with filter and sort controls redrawn to match the app.
- Moved the episode list onto the details page and read studio, producers, score and age rating from extension descriptions.
- Trimmed the empty banner area on titles without a banner.

**Profile**
- Library-based achievements are now kept once earned, use the same names and conditions as the desktop app and open a detail sheet with every level.

**Fixes**
- Fixed a false "no internet" message right after unlocking the phone.
- Fixed duplicate titles in Saved, Downloads and the continue-watching row.
- Fixed back with the keyboard open leaving the search instead of hiding the keyboard.
- Fixed a startup crash in release builds and the upcoming-episode number in the episode list.
