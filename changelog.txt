## hibiki 2.8.1

**Sources**
- A Cloudflare check that cannot be solved automatically can now be completed by hand in a built-in WebView, opened from the error screens of Home, Catalog and Search.
- Covers of sources behind Cloudflare load again.

**Playback**
- Fixed resuming from a saved position and seeking on AnimePahe, where playback used to load forever.
- Fixed playback of streams that an extension serves through its own local proxy.

**Interface**
- Fixed the Home loading placeholder overlapping the "Trending now" heading.
