## hibiki 2.8.2

**Downloads**
- Episodes now download with one tap: best available quality and every subtitle track, saved for offline playback.
- A download shows what it is doing (finding the stream, checking it, downloading) instead of a bare "Queued", and can be cancelled while it is still being prepared.
- Fixed downloads that stayed in the queue forever when a source did not respond.

**Sources**
- Extensions no longer fail on responses with missing optional fields, which broke KickAssAnime's Home.
- Fixed the endless "Installing..." spinner after allowing installs from unknown apps.
- Sources behind Cloudflare can be verified ahead of time from the extension menu, and the challenge reopens with the failing request's own headers.
- Playback errors now say why (rate limit, source unavailable), and the app backs off from a source that answers with HTTP 429.

**Interface**
- Year filters are shown as a slider again.
- Home no longer flashes "Go to sources" before the offline error.
- Episode lists show a loading shimmer.
